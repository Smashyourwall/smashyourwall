Work With Me

If you've spent months or years wanting to approach women but keep finding yourself freezing, overthinking, or walking away, this is what I help with.

I've been there.

I spent years knowing exactly what I wanted to do and still being unable to make myself do it.

Now I help other men work through the same problem.

What I Help With

  • Getting yourself to actually walk over instead of spending 20 minutes thinking about it
  • Breaking the hesitation that takes over when the moment arrives
  • Befriending rejection and becoming comfortable hearing no
  • Approaching when other people are watching
  • Building confidence through experience instead of trying to convince yourself you're confident

You wouldn't expect to feel confident playing basketball if you'd never stepped on a court.

Approaching works the same way.

Confidence comes from accumulating evidence that you can actually do the thing.

Who This IS For

  • ✓ Your career and finances are in a reasonable place, but your dating life is way behind where you want it to be.
  • ✓ You're an action taker. You're willing to actually implement what we work on rather than just collect more advice.
  • ✓ You're in a position where investing $1,500 in solving this problem is realistic for you.
  • ✓ You're willing to practice in the real world, knowing you'll have someone in your corner who will help you work through it without judging you.
  • ✓ You're open to practicing during the day, rather than making nightlife the only place you're willing to approach.

Who This IS NOT For

  • ❌ You want pre-recorded "pickup" content to binge and never apply. This is not a course. It's built around you and your situation.
  • ❌ You're too busy to invest the time and get out there for a few hours a week.
  • ❌ You're currently struggling financially and $1,500 would put you under financial pressure. If that's the case, coaching probably isn't your priority right now.
  • ❌ You're completely unwilling to practice outside of nightlife. I'm not interested in building this around bars, clubs, alcohol, and waiting until nighttime to approach.
  • ❌ You're looking for manipulation, tricks, or a way to guarantee women saying yes.
