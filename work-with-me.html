Work With Me

Stop Thinking About Approaching.
Start Actually Approaching.

If you've spent months or years wanting to approach women but keep finding yourself freezing, overthinking, or walking away, this is what I help with.

I've been there.

I spent years knowing exactly what I wanted to do and still being unable to make myself do it.

I knew I wanted to approach women. I knew I was capable of doing it. And yet when the moment came, my brain would say go while my body would say absolutely not.

Eventually, I stopped trying to think my way out of it and started doing the thing I was afraid of.

Now I help other men work through the same problem.

What I Help With

→ Getting yourself to actually walk over instead of spending 20 minutes thinking about it
→ Breaking hesitation and learning how to act before your fear talks you out of it
→ Befriending rejection instead of treating it like something you need to avoid
→ Approaching when other people are watching
→ Building confidence through actual experience instead of consuming more information

You wouldn't expect to feel confident playing basketball if you'd never stepped on a court.

Approaching works the same way.

Confidence comes from accumulating evidence that you can actually do the thing.

Is This Actually For You?

I don't think coaching makes sense for everyone. This works best when you're already in a position to put the work in and actually want to change this part of your life.

This Makes Sense If...

  • Your career and finances are in a reasonable place, but your dating life is way behind where you want it to be.
  • You're an action taker and are willing to implement rather than collect more advice.
  • A $1,500 investment is realistic for you.
  • You're willing to practice in the real world with someone in your corner who won't judge you.
  • You're open to practicing during the day rather than relying exclusively on nightlife.

Probably Not A Fit If...

  • You want pre-recorded "pickup" content to binge without actually applying it. This isn't a course. It's built around you.
  • You're too busy to invest the time and get out a few hours a week to practice.
  • You're financially struggling and spending $1,500 would create pressure. Coaching probably isn't the priority right now.
  • You're completely unwilling to practice outside of nightlife. I'm not interested in building this around bars, clubs, alcohol, or late nights.
  • You want manipulation, tricks, or a guarantee that women will say yes.
