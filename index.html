Actually Approach Women

The goal isn't to become some completely different person.

It's to be able to see a woman you're interested in, decide you want to talk to her, and actually walk over and do it.

No waiting for the perfect moment. No spending twenty minutes talking yourself into it.

Just going and accepting whatever happens, happens.

Learn to Handle Rejection

She might say no. That's part of it.

The goal isn't to figure out how to make every woman say yes. The goal is to get to the point where hearing no doesn't stop you from approaching the next woman you actually want to talk to.

Make Approaching Feel Normal

Approaching women can feel like defusing a bomb at first.

But the more you do something, the less foreign it becomes. Eventually, the thing that once seemed impossible can become something you simply do.
