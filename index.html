MY STORY

I Was Sick of Being Stuck

Around 2018, online dating started feeling completely different.

At least for me, it wasn't working.

I could spend weeks swiping, matching with women, having conversations that went nowhere, and occasionally getting a date.

And then I'd go right back to doing the same thing!

I'd seen guys on YouTube walking up to women they didn't know, starting conversations, getting numbers, getting dates.

They made it look almost ridiculous how simple it was.

Just walk over, say hello, and talk to her.

But whenever I imagined myself doing it, it seemed completely impossible.

I was scared.

And for a long time, I did what was probably the easiest thing I could do:

Nothing.

I was sick of relying on dating apps and WAITING months for another date while watching other people do something that seemed completely impossible for me.

And more than anything, I was sick of knowing exactly what I wanted to do and still being unable to make myself do it.

I reached a breaking point, a rock bottom if you will.

I decided I was going to figure this out.

I couldn't keep doing what I'd been doing.

So I made myself a promise:

I was going to approach.

And eventually, that promise led me to October 6, 2019.
