COACHING FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are answers to common questions about coaching and what to expect.

What if I've already watched tons of dating/cold approach content?

Doing and knowing are 2 different things, but having an idea of what you're getting into is somewhat advantageous. You already have an idea of what the process looks like, and therefore you know what your life can look like. I help people who are knowledgeable but can't actually put what they know into practice for whatever reason.

Do we actually approach women during coaching?

Yes! I do not just sit around with a microphone giving PowerPoint presentations. Depending on which program fits your situation best, you will be getting unfiltered, real-world feedback either way.

Why do you emphasize approaching during the day?

I personally enjoy it the most. I hate clubs and nightlife in general. During the day, you can get their undivided attention with minimal distractions and can actually hear them. You can have a real conversation instead of trying to yell over music and compete with everything else happening around you. Can you get that at a club?

Is coaching remote or in person?

Depending on your situation and preference, I offer both. Remote coaching typically has 2 calls a week for 1 hour (though they will more likely be 1.5–2 hours depending on how much you actually have to discuss) and in-person coaching will be anywhere from 4–8 hours at a high-traffic, popular mall or something like that. Volume will not be an issue.

What happens when I choose/register for the program?

Once you register, we will get everything scheduled. We typically get started on the first available day you are able to talk with me and go from there.

Can approach anxiety actually go away?

Oh hell yes! I would know as someone who had to get out there and couldn't approach for years. For me, it took about 30 approaches before I noticed it was mostly on its way out of my life. It felt like I was a completely different person with some massive upgrades. For you it might take more or less. I can't guarantee it will disappear, but I can guarantee that if you seriously work at it and make an effort to approach, it will be far, far less severe than when you started.

Will I ever get to the point where approaching feels normal?

Yes. But "normal" doesn't necessarily mean you will never feel nervous again. It means approaching will stop feeling like this massive event that you have to mentally prepare yourself for. You will be able to see a woman you want to talk to, decide to approach, and just go do it. You will be taking out chunks of your approach anxiety wall every time you put yourself in that situation and walk through it unscathed. Eventually, it just won't feel like such a huge deal anymore.

How long does it take to become comfortable approaching?

This is going to vary a lot from person to person. The number of approaches matters, but so does how consistently you do them and how much you're actually pushing yourself. For me, I noticed a major shift after about 30 approaches. Someone else might need 10. Someone else might need 100. I wouldn't obsess over the number. The important thing is that you're consistently getting out there and putting yourself in situations that make you uncomfortable.

What if I have very little dating experience?

You have to start somewhere. You don't need years of dating experience before you can start approaching women. If you're starting from almost nothing, that's okay. You have a lot of room to improve, and that's exactly what the coaching is for. The sky is the limit!

What if I've never approached a woman before?

Honestly, I would say most men haven't cold approached during the day. So if you've never done it before, you're not some weird exception. You're just a beginner. Everyone who is good at something was once terrible at it, and approaching is no different. You don't need to know how to do it before you start. That's part of what you're here to learn.

How long is each coaching call?

Anywhere from 60–120 minutes generally. It depends on how much stuff we have to talk about. Some calls may be closer to an hour, while others may run longer because there is simply more to work through.

How often are the coaching calls?

2 times a week. That gives you enough time between calls to actually go out and practice while keeping enough contact that you don't disappear for a week or two and lose momentum. The calls give us something to work with, so the expectation is that you'll actually go out, take action, and come back with real experiences to discuss.

What do we actually talk about on the calls?

Everything. The first call is mostly about you. We will discuss who you are, your experience, your personality, what you've tried before, and where you're currently getting stuck. I want to understand you as an individual before deciding what I think you should be doing. From there, we'll figure out the best course of action for you.

Will you hold me accountable?

Yes. You are paying me. If you think I'm just going to take your money and casually check in with you every once in a while, you will be mistaken. I'm going to do everything I can to help you change your life when it comes to women and share the skill set I've developed over years of doing this myself.

Can I tell you about approaches that went badly?

Yes. There will be “bad” ones at some point, however you want to define that. The biggest change will be that the thought of a “bad approach” no longer paralyzes you. Eventually, a bad approach will just be a bad approach. You'll learn from it, move on, and probably forget about it. Your brain won't have to spend hours replaying what you said, what she said, what you should have said, or what everyone around you might have thought. You'll have more important things to focus on.

How much time should I expect to spend practicing each week?

Bare minimum, 2 times a week. When I started, I was going out 3 times a week, and 2 of the sessions were typically 5+ hours on the weekends because I loved it so much. I was so committed to changing my life and expanding my options that I was taking the train 1.5 hours each way! More is better generally, but if you can't do it more than 2 times a week, I don't think you're giving yourself enough opportunities to build and maintain momentum. If you went to the gym only 1 time a week, you wouldn't be going often enough to really see your results improve, right?

What happens when the program is over?

Honestly, when the program is over, you will probably feel like a different person with many beliefs having shifted immensely in ways you couldn't have predicted before. You will obviously retain access to me. I won't just suddenly cut ties with you, but if all goes well, you will have put in enough volume and I will have helped you enough that you won't need me the way you did when you started. That's the goal.

How do I know which coaching option is right for me?

It really depends on who you are.

In-person Wall Smasher: This is for the more adventurous type, unless you already happen to be in Medellin. It's for the guy who is sick of making excuses, tired of watching his dating life stay far below what he knows it could be, and is ready to stop thinking about approaching and actually do it. Maybe you've wanted to approach for years, but you've never been able to consistently get yourself to take action. All that built-up potential is ready to burst like water behind an old dam. Sometimes the best option is to finally say, “Fuck this,” jump into the deep end, and see what happens.

Online Program: This is for someone who is sick of making excuses but wants me there with him for 6 weeks straight, helping him through the roller coaster of emotions that comes with actually doing this. It's also the option for someone who isn't available to travel to another country and do it in person.

Sustained effort over time will win, of course, but doing it in person with someone physically there watching and guiding you has obvious advantages. Online calls can still be extremely effective, but I'm ultimately limited to the information you give me during our calls and what you are able to communicate about your experiences.

If I pay $1,500 for the in-person 1-day, and then decide I really want to do the 3-day, does that original $1,500 payment count toward the 3-day total or do I have to pay separately?

The $1,500 can be used as credit toward the 3-day Wall Smasher if you decide you want to continue after the 1-day and keep building on the momentum you've gained.
