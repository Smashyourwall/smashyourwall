Coaching FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about coaching, approach anxiety, and what you can expect if we work together.

Still Have Questions?

Here are some of the questions I get most often about approach anxiety, coaching, and what working together actually looks like.

What if I've already watched tons of dating/cold approach content?

Doing and knowing are two different things.

Having an idea of what the process looks like is somewhat advantageous. You already have an idea of what the process looks like and therefore you know what your life can be like.

I help people who are knowledgeable but can't do it for whatever reason. If you've already watched a ton of content but still find yourself freezing when the moment comes, more information probably isn't what you need.

Do we actually approach women during coaching?

Yes.

The whole point is to actually do the thing you're afraid of. We'll spend time talking through what's happening, but we'll also get into the real world and approach.

I don't want you spending hours talking about approaching without actually approaching.

Why do you emphasize approaching during the day?

Because I think daytime environments give you a much better environment for learning how to approach.

It's easier to actually hear each other, there isn't alcohol or club noise involved, and you aren't relying on a nightlife environment to create opportunities.

That's how I learned, and it's the environment I prefer to teach in.

Is coaching remote or in person?

Both.

The 1-on-1 6 Week Transformation is online. The Wall Smasher options are in person and involve actually getting out into the real world together.

If you're interested in something custom, we can discuss what makes sense for your situation.

What happens when I choose/register for the program?

Once you register, we will get everything scheduled. For online, we typically get started on the first available day you are able to talk with me and go from there. For in person, we will coordinate schedules and locations.

Can approach anxiety actually go away?

I don't think you need to wait for fear to completely disappear before you can approach.

What changes is your relationship with the fear. Something that once felt enormous can become something you know how to deal with. The goal is to get to the point where approaching isn't this huge event that controls your behavior.

Will I ever get to the point where approaching feels normal?

Yes, I believe it can become much more normal.

That doesn't mean you'll never feel nervous. It means the nerves don't have to control what you do.

Something that once required a huge amount of mental effort can eventually become something you simply do.

How long does it take to become comfortable approaching?

This is going to vary a lot from person to person. The number of approaches matters, but so does how consistently you do them and how much you're actually pushing yourself.

For me, I noticed a major shift after about 30 approaches. Someone else might need 10. Someone else might need 50.

I wouldn't obsess over the number. The important thing is that you're consistently getting out there and putting yourself in situations that make you uncomfortable. Over time and with reps, it won't seem like such a big deal.

What if I have very little dating experience?

That's okay.

You don't need a long dating history to work on approach anxiety. In fact, learning how to get comfortable taking the first step can be one of the most useful things you can do if you don't have much experience yet.

What if I've never approached a woman before?

That's okay too.

You don't need to have experience before starting. We'll work from wherever you're actually at rather than pretending you're further along than you are.

How long is each coaching call?

The calls are designed around what we actually need to work on, rather than trying to force every conversation into an arbitrary length.

How often are the coaching calls?

We'll figure out a schedule that makes sense for you and gives you enough time between calls to actually practice.

The goal isn't to spend all your time talking to me. The goal is to use our conversations to help you take action outside of them.

What do we actually talk about on the calls?

We talk about what's actually happening for you.

That could mean hesitation, overthinking, fear of rejection, what happened during a particular approach, what you're avoiding, or whatever else is getting in your way.

The more you actually practice between calls, the more specific and useful our conversations can become.

Will you hold me accountable?

Yes.

If you're paying me to help you break through this, I don't want the coaching to become another place where you talk about what you're going to do without doing it.

I'll expect you to actually get out there and practice.

Can I tell you about approaches that went badly?

Absolutely.

Bad approaches are part of the process. You can tell me what happened, we can look at it honestly, and figure out what you can learn from it rather than letting one bad experience stop you from continuing.

How much time should I expect to spend practicing each week?

It depends on your schedule and your goals, but you should expect to actually practice.

Even a few hours a week can give us something to work with. The important part is consistency and actually putting yourself in situations that make you uncomfortable.

What happens when the program is over?

The goal isn't to make you dependent on coaching.

The goal is for you to have enough experience that you can keep going on your own. Ideally, approaching becomes something you know how to handle rather than a problem you need someone else to solve for you.

How do I know which coaching option is right for me?

That depends on how much support and real-world practice you're looking for.

The 6 Week Transformation is designed for ongoing online support. The 1-Day and 3-Day Wall Smasher options are for people who want concentrated in-person practice.

If you're unsure, that's exactly what the application is for. Tell me about your situation and we can figure out what makes sense.

If I pay $1,500 for the in-person 1-day, and then decide I really want to do the 3-day, does that original $1,500 payment count toward the 3-day total or do I have to pay separately?

If you start with the 1-Day Wall Smasher and decide you want to continue into the 3-Day Wall Smasher, the original $1,500 payment would count toward the $3,500 total.

You would simply pay the remaining $2,000.
