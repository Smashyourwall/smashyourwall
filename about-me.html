About Me

I wasn't naturally smooth. I was hyper-analytical and totally frozen.

My name is Phil. For years, my life made zero sense on paper. I could sit at high-stakes poker tables for hours, make cold calculated decisions under intense pressure, compete in sports, and operate with total logic—until I saw an attractive woman walking past me in broad daylight.

Then my brain would shut down. My heart rate would spike, I’d rationalize twenty reasons why "now isn't the right time," and I'd watch the opportunity walk away. Nothing.

Knowing what you ought to do and being physically able to walk over and do it are two entirely different skill sets.

The "Grandpa Method" vs. Modern Pickup Nonsense

When I finally got sick of my own excuses, I tried learning how to fix it. But almost everything in the dating space was insufferable—cringey pick-up line scripts, fake "alpha male" posturing, or weird manipulative tricks designed to hide insecurity behind a character.

I didn't want a character. I just wanted to be able to see a woman I thought was attractive, walk over, start a normal conversation like a human being, and accept whatever happened next.

Before smartphones, that wasn't called "cold approach." It was just how people met. I started calling it the Grandpa Method—getting comfortable taking action in the real world without needing liquor, dark nightclubs, or weird games.

What Actually Happens When You Smash The Wall

I didn't solve this through a magical mindset shift or reading another book. I solved it through volume. Thousands of real-world interactions, direct approaches in plain daylight, hundreds of awkward rejections, and building undeniable proof that the sky doesn't fall when a girl says no.

The weirdest side effect? Once you conquer approach anxiety, you stop obsessing over women. The needy, desperate energy vanishes. You realize that getting comfortable with hesitation gives you control over every other uncomfortable area of your life.

I don't teach tricks. I help guys who are smart and capable everywhere else in life finally break through the wall holding their social life back.
