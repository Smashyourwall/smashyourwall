Break the Wall

The story of how I spent three years terrified to approach women—and what happened when I finally made the decision to stop running in fear.

I was terrified to approach. I broke through it. I can help you do the same.

For years, I wanted to approach women.

I watched videos. I imagined myself doing it.

And then, when the moment actually came, I couldn't do it.

I spent years walking past women I wanted to talk to, becoming a master of excuses, and wondering why something that seemed so simple for other people felt almost impossible for me.

Eventually, I reached a point where I couldn't keep living that way.

October 6, 2019

I finally approached.

I had had enough. And it went terribly.

Trust me, that first approach didn't magically fix everything. In fact, it was only the beginning.

What followed was years of approaches, rejection, uncomfortable conversations, unexpected successes, mistakes, and a lot of figuring things out the hard way.

I wrote the whole story down.

Break the Wall is the unfiltered account of how I went from being terrified to approach women to eventually making it something I could actually do.

No course. No sales pitch. Just my story.
